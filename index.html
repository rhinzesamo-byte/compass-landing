Week 1 · CLEAR

Clear Mind + Clear Heart

Mon: a brain dump sorted into Hawak Ko · Hindi Ko Hawak · Hindi Akin. You name one fear and clear one small corner.
Wed: name 3 feelings, write your energy lists and a private money snapshot, and finish the line “Gusto kong maramdaman sa 2027 na…”

Your fears, money and heavy feelings are never asked for in the group.

you leave with

  • A sorted brain dump + one named fear
  • Feelings & energy lists

Week 2 · FIND · West + North

What You Are Good At + What You Love

Mon: list 10+ strengths and ask 3 people, “Kailan mo ako nakitang nasa best ko?”
Wed: list 10+ loves, then do one loved thing for 30 minutes before Friday. (15 minutes still counts.)

you leave with

  • “Ang galing ko ay…”
  • “Ang mahal ko ay…, at magaling ako sa…”

Week 3 · FIND · East + South

What the World Needs + What You Can Be Paid For

Mon: the problems and people you care about, plus one 15–20 minute listening conversation.
Wed: map three paths of value — your current job, a career shift, or your own small offer — and run one small test. No quitting, no spending, no announcements.

you leave with

  • Your full Compass statement
  • One tested possibility

Week 4 · ACT → BECOME

Clear Plan + Clear Action

Mon: one priority, a WOOP (Wish, Outcome, Obstacle, Plan), four weekly steps with day, time and place, and one support person.
Wed: you start your First Action during the lesson and finish it within 48 hours. Fri: graduation.

After the course, I check in with you on Day 15 and Day 30.

you leave with

When does the course start?

The dates for the founding batch aren’t set yet. They’ll be announced to everyone who enrolls in this batch before Week 1.

How much time do I need each week?

About 20–45 minutes for each Monday and Wednesday lesson and activity, plus the 55-minute Friday call. Some activities include a small real-life task, like one 15–20 minute conversation. Weekends are free.

What if I miss a Friday call?

Every call is recorded and shared with the batch that night. Watching the recording counts toward the guarantee.

Do I have to share my fears or money situation with the group?

No. Your fears, money and heavy feelings are never asked for in the group. Those parts of the workbook stay private. Sharing on calls is volunteers-first, and you can pass.

Is this therapy?

No — this is coaching, not therapy. If you’re in crisis, please reach the NCMH Crisis Hotline at 1553, 0917-899-8727 or 0919-057-1553 (24/7), or call 911.

Will this help me earn more money?

I don’t promise income or give financial advice. In Week 3, you explore three paths of value — your current job, a career shift, or your own small offer — and every path is valid.

How do I pay, and when do I get access?

Scan the QR with GCash, Maya or any bank app that supports QR Ph / InstaPay, then fill in the reservation form with your reference number and a screenshot of your payment. I check each payment by hand. Once it’s confirmed, your welcome message and course access arrive by email.

What do I need?

A Google account (for your workbook and the Friday calls on Google Meet), a phone or laptop, and the willingness to do the activities. Using AI is optional.

How does the refund guarantee work?

If you submit the activities for all 8 lessons, attend or watch all 4 calls, and attempt your First Action — and still have no clear direction — you get a refund. Requests are accepted until 7 days after graduation.
